# Delete your ardeo account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from inside the app.

---

## How

Open **Settings** in the app and tap **Delete account**. Confirming signs you
out immediately, and your account is fully removed from our servers within 30
days.

If you've lost your phone, or lost access to the address you signed in with,
email **[privacy@ardeo.nz](mailto:privacy@ardeo.nz)** from the address you
signed in with, with the subject **Delete my account**. If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your
email address, say so in the message and include your display name — we will
verify another way rather than ask you to reveal it.

---

## What gets deleted

- Your profile, display name, handle and photo
- Every walk you recorded, including its statistics and note
- The coverage squares recorded against your walks
- The walks you planned for a group, and your answers saying you were coming
- Anything else you wrote in the app

A group you own passes straight away to the admin who has been in it longest.
If it has no admin, the group — and everyone's membership in it — is deleted
along with your account.

There is no stored route line to delete. ardeo never uploads one — see the
[privacy policy](./privacy.md).

Deleting in the app also clears your walks, routes included, off that phone.
If you delete by email instead, whatever is on your phone goes when you
delete the app, or when someone else signs in to ardeo on it.

## What does not get deleted

**The coverage counts you contributed remain.**

A coverage count records that an area was prayed over on a given day. It has
no person attached to it and cannot be traced back to you, so those counts
stay. Other people's records of their own neighbourhoods are built from the
same totals, so removing yours would corrupt theirs.

**Reports about you are kept.** If other walkers reported you, or a walk or
planned walk of yours, those reports stay after your account is gone: the
reason they chose, what they wrote, and the id of what they reported, which
leads to nothing once your account is removed. Each report was also emailed
to us when it was made, naming you by the display name and handle you had
then, and those emails stay in our inbox. Reports you sent are kept too, no
longer linked to you.

Apart from those reports, nothing that identifies you survives in our
database once removal completes.

---

## Timing

Deleting your account in the app is immediate: you're signed out straight
away, and removal from our servers completes within 30 days. If you email us
instead, the same 30-day removal starts once we've confirmed it's really you,
which we aim to do within a few days.

After you delete in the app, the address you signed in with can't be used to
sign in to ardeo until removal completes: a sign-in link sent to it is
refused. So it can't make a new account in that time either. Once removal
completes, the address is free to make a new account.

Copies outside the live database take a little longer. Our providers keep
service logs, which can include your email address and IP address, for up to
30 days, and Supabase's own backups roll over on its schedule. After those
periods, no copy that identifies you remains, apart from the reports above.
