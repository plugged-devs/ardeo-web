Sacred Ground & Personal Safety

Our Privacy Charter

GPS recording touches homes, routines, and families. Ardeo was crafted with non-negotiable architectural privacy from day one.

Commitment 01

Coarse coverage, never raw paths.

Your minute-by-minute trajectory is never uploaded or published. Only aggregate covered street segments are lit on the communal map to safeguard your doorstep.

Commitment 02

You choose who sees what.

Every single walk can be completely private, shared strictly with verified companions, or contributed to your parish field. Switch visibility whenever you like.

Commitment 03

Zero ads, zero data brokers.

We do not run advertising networks, monetize location telemetry, or harvest intercessory notes. Your prayers and physical steps remain sacred.
