How a walk works

  1. Start a walk

    Tap the flame and put your phone away. ardeo records the route while you pray.

  2. Mark what you're praying for

    Stopped outside a school or a church? Pick what you're praying for, add a note if you like, and keep walking.

  3. See the streets you covered

    When you finish, the streets you walked shade in on the map. Switch between everyone's prayer and just your own.

Walk with your church

Start a group for your church, small group or ministry team. Share walks to the group and see the ground you've covered together.

What ardeo keeps, and what it doesn't

A prayer walk is a record of where you were. We built ardeo so that record stays as small as it can be.

Your route stays on your phone
The line you walked is never uploaded, at any setting. Our server has nowhere to store one.
Free to use
ardeo costs nothing to use, with no paid tier. It was initiated by Plugged Worship, an NZ-based Korean missional worship group.
Private means private
A private walk is seen by nobody but you. Your marks and their photos never leave your phone.
No ads, no tracking
We don't sell your data, show ads, or put any analytics in the app. Sign in with Apple, Google or an emailed link.
